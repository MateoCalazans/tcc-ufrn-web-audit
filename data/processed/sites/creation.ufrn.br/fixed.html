ABOUT

CREATION is a multidisciplinary research center, leader in Brazil in wind energy, mainly offshore wind and its applications in Power-to-X technologies. With more than 15 years of experience in RD&I, our mission is to disseminate knowledge that contributes to the sustainable development. We are driven by curiosity of generating knowledge aligned with the sustainability of human life on the planet.
