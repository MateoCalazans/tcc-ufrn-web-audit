PROGRAM

All scheduled events will take place virtually via the ZOOM platform

Forum Connect RN is a platform to promote the research, development and innovation ecosystem of the State of Rio Grande do Norte (Brazil) and leverage discussion and knowledge exchange on global challenges of the current and future times, creating opportunities for international collaboration to develop innovative solutions. To achieve this, a multi-disciplinary approach is key to building the path to a resilient and sustainable city, where local government prioritises nature and people on a disaster and risks prevention and social equity approach. A joint effort that requires the engagement of the public and private sectors, along with the local communities, the young people and the research and innovation ecosystem to promote a just and fair transition to a greener, healthier, safer, and happier society.

Panel 2: Paving the way to Resilient and Sustainable Cities

A multi-disciplinary approach is key to building the path to a resilient and sustainable city, where the local government prioritises nature and people on a disaster and risks prevention and social equity approach. A joint effort that requires the engagement of the public and private sectors, along with the local communities, the young people and the research and innovation ecosystem to promote a just and fair transition to a greener, healthier, safer and happier society.

The panel will explore how other Latin American cities with the RDI ecosystem are making their way to become more resilient and sustainable in face of global challenges posed by Climate Change and world pandemics. With a focus on experiences addressing natural disasters prevention such as flooding and drought, unemployment, and ways of addressing the panellists shed light on:

  • Why regions should address global challenges in their journey to become resilient and how innovation and international collaboration can bring about change and effective solutions;
  • Why we need to embed global challenges innovation in local decision-making and how the RD&I ecosystems can promote more efficient, fairer and sustainable responses;
  • How can local citizens engage on this journey and become change makers?

FAQ

Who is promoting the event?
This event is being organized by the Potiguar Internationalization Network which is composed of the Federal University of Rio Grande do Norte, SEBRAE, FIERN, City Hall of Natal, Government of Rio Grande do Norte, Metrópole Digital Tech Park, and PAX.
How will the event happen?
The event will be online by the ZOOM platform. The language of the event will be English. There will be a translation from portuguese to english and english to portuguese.
How can I subscribe?
You can subscribe for both days of the event through this link.
How much does the registration for the event cost?
The event is all free.
Who can subscribe to the event?
Everyone interested in overcoming, in a collaborative and innovative way, the current and future global challenges in areas of excellence of the Potiguar ecosystem and in promoting future international cooperation.
Are there any requirements to participate in the event?
No.
Is there any additional contact?
In case of doubts or more details, please contact thercio@imd.ufrn.br.
